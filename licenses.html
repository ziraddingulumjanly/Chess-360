Stockfish 19 and Stockfish.js

Chess 360 ships the full Stockfish 19 multithreaded WebAssembly engine and its JavaScript loader. Stockfish/Stockfish.js are licensed under the GNU GPLv3. The full engine is distributed with Stockfish GPLv3 text, Stockfish.js GPLv3 text, Stockfish source archive, and Stockfish.js source archive.

Original repositories: Stockfish and Stockfish.js. Distributors must verify complete corresponding source for the exact binary and comply with the GPLv3.

chess.js

Legal move handling uses the BSD-licensed chess.js library by Jeff Hlywa, bundled locally. Source.

Chess piece theme gallery

The Settings gallery includes 2 traditional shaded variants of Merida, 7 Chess Art collections by Maurizio Monge (MIT) and 10 independently licensed collections sourced from Lichess. Each collection is credited to its actual artist, not generically to Lichess.
