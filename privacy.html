Privacy Policy

How the locally running Chess 360 application handles browser data.

Review before public deployment: This policy documents the application as supplied. The website operator should add their legal identity, contact details and hosting-specific disclosures before publishing.

Data stored locally

Chess 360 stores your preferences, your current game, the 40 most recent completed games, and aggregate statistics through the browser's localStorage. This data stays in the same browser/profile unless you manually export it, copy PGN, or your browser syncs it through its own features.

Computer opponent

Stockfish 19 runs inside your device's browser. Chess moves are not sent to an AI API or a remote game server by this application's gameplay code. The full engine's files are fetched from the same website when needed and may be cached by your browser.

Accounts, analytics and cookies

This version has no accounts, no Supabase, no application analytics, and no advertising trackers. It does not deliberately set cookies. Read the Cookie Policy.

Hosting and external links

A production web host may independently process IP addresses and access logs to deliver the site. Its retention practices depend on the host, which has not been specified here. Links to GitHub and open-source project sites load those third-party services only when selected; those sites have their own privacy practices.

Delete your local data

To erase saved Chess 360 games and preferences, clear the site's data/storage in your browser settings. There is no account deletion process because no accounts are created.
