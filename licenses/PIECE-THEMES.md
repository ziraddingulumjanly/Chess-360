# Chess 360 — Piece artwork credits and reuse conditions

Piece artwork is copyright its original creators. Each theme is **independently licensed**. Links and license notices below are provided for convenience; preserve the relevant notices when redistributing. Except for artwork from the original Chess 360 set, these are unmodified redistributions of the uploaded sources; the dynamically recolored Fantasy, Celtic, and Spatial SVGs are transformed in the browser for display.

## Original Chess 360 pieces

- **Original** — SVG silhouettes vector-traced from Cburnett/Wikipedia-style chess graphics. Original artist: **Colin M. L. Burnett (Cburnett)**. [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Existing `NOTICE.txt` provides the original attribution.

## Traditional Staunton-style finishes — derived from Merida

- **Classic Ivory** and **Classic Wood** — locally generated shaded PNG interpretations of the **Merida** SVG silhouettes by **Armando Hernandez Marroquin**, as distributed in the Lichess piece collection. These are **adaptations**, with color, shadow and shading changes, and are offered under the original **GPLv2 or later** terms. [Merida source attribution](Lichess-COPYING.md); [GPLv2 license text](GPLv2-Piece-Themes.txt). All 24 rendered pieces are included under `assets/piece-themes/staunton-*/*.png`, derived from `assets/piece-themes/lila-merida/*.svg`.

## Chess Art — Maurizio Monge

- **Fantasy, Celtic, Spatial, Skulls, Eyes, Prmi, Freak** — **Maurizio Monge**, [Chess Art GitHub](https://github.com/maurimo/chess-art), [MIT](Chess-Art-MIT.txt). Fantasy, Celtic and Spatial are displayed with optional customized gradients, outlines, and shadows. The Fantasy, Celtic and Spatial default prebuilt variants were included in Lichess's public/piece collection and likewise credited there as Chess Art MIT work. Skulls, Eyes, Prmi, and Freak are taken directly from `chess-art-main/fancy/`.
