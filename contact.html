Contact & feedback

Project contact details and how to report an issue.

Website support

Chess 360 has no website-owner contact address configured in the provided source. Before publishing, the operator should add a real support email or a dedicated project issue tracker here. No contact form is shown because the website has no backend to receive messages.

Feedback checklist

For a chess UI bug, include your browser/version, device, the moves leading to the problem, and—if possible—a PGN copied from the game's controls. Avoid including private information.

Engine-specific issues

If a problem relates to Stockfish itself, the upstream Stockfish.js issue tracker may be useful. This is an independent project and not an official Chess 360 support channel.

Legal/privacy questions

The operator should supply their contact details on this page before deploying the public website. See the Privacy Policy and Terms.
