What the service offers

Chess 360 provides local chess gameplay against an open-source Stockfish engine. Results, availability and performance can vary by device, browser and hardware. No accounts, online matchmaking or paid features are included in this version.

Acceptable use

Use the service lawfully. Do not attempt to abuse hosting infrastructure, interfere with its availability or misrepresent the project as an official Stockfish or Chess.com product.

Availability and data

Gameplay is provided as-is, without guarantees of uninterrupted availability. Browser data may be lost if storage is cleared, evicted or disabled. Save PGN records elsewhere if you need a long-term archive.
